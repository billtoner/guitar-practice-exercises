# Week 1 — Seventh-Chord Arpeggios (m7 · dom7 · maj7)

> **Phase 1 · Foundations of automaticity.** Part of [The Recall Gap](the-recall-gap-plan.md) 12-week plan.

**This week's one goal:** find the tones of any **m7, dom7, or maj7** chord from its **6th- and 5th-string roots**, *named → played in under a second.* No speed, no solos yet — just converting three shapes from "I know that" into "my hand already went there."

**Why this first:** every chord in smooth jazz is a flavor of these three. If you can't grab their tones instantly, nothing downstream (targeting, guide-tone lines, improv) can happen in real time. This is the taproot.

---

## The three shapes (a 30-second refresher)

Spelled from the root, the only difference between them is the **3rd and the 7th** — the two "guide tones" that define a chord's sound. Burn this in; it's the whole of Week 2:

| Chord | Formula | 3rd | 7th |
|---|---|---|---|
| **m7** (minor 7) | 1 · ♭3 · 5 · ♭7 | flat | flat |
| **dom7** (7) | 1 · 3 · 5 · ♭7 | natural | flat |
| **maj7** | 1 · 3 · 5 · 7 | natural | natural |

Read down the 3rd/7th columns: **m7 → dom7** raises the 3rd; **dom7 → maj7** raises the 7th. Two notes move; the root and 5th never do. That is the entire difference between "minor," "dominant," and "major seventh." Say it out loud until it's boring.

**The two shapes each week:** one arpeggio with its **root on the 6th (low E) string**, one with its **root on the 5th (A) string**. Let Fretsy's neck diagram (Degrees display) show you the shape — you're training the *visual + physical* pattern, so watch the R–3–5–7 light up rather than reading fret numbers off a page.

## The daily session (30–45 min)

### ① Warm-up · Fretboard flash — 5 min · **Drills tab**
- **Name the Note**, then **Find the Note**.
- Timer: **Beat the Clock** — start at **10 s**, tighten toward **5 s** by Day 7.
- Spoken micro-drill: say a random 7th chord aloud ("E♭maj7"), spell **R–3–5–7** *before* you look. This trains the naming half of "named → played."

### ② Reflex drill — 10 min · **Practice tab** *(the core of the week)*
- **Lock Key** to today's key (from the table).
- **Lock Root string to the 6th** for the first 5 min, then switch the lock to the **5th** for the next 5.
- Pattern **Straight** · Display **Degrees** (first half) → **Notes** (second half) · Neck **ON** · Zoom **single position**.
- Direction per the day's row above.
- **Recall test:** turn on **Hands-free auto-advance** with the countdown at **~4 s**, tightening to **2 s**. A new random arpeggio appears; the win is being **on the root before the count-in ends**, then playing it clean.

### ③ Apply — 10–15 min · **Practice tab** *(make it musical + connect shapes)*
- Same content, but **Loop ON**, Pattern **In 3rds** → **Groups of 4**, Direction **Both**, Zoom **whole neck**.
- Goal: stop playing isolated boxes — **connect the 6th- and 5th-string shapes** into one arpeggio that spans the neck.
- Tempo **70–90 BPM**.
- *Improv seed (optional but valuable):* pull up an external m7 vamp backing track (YouTube / iReal Pro) in today's key and noodle using **only** the arpeggio tones. First real improv rep. *(This is exactly the in-app gap Fretsy's planned "Changes Mode" will fill.)*

### ④ Ear + steal — 5–10 min · **Drills tab**
- **Chord Quality by Ear**, limited to **m7 / dom7 / maj7** — learn to *hear* the three qualities you're drilling (listen for whether the 3rd and 7th sound "flat" or "natural").
- Then **steal**: play one short Benson or Peter White phrase and find which chord tone each note lands on.

---

## Tempo ladder (blocks ② and ③)

```
Start:   60 BPM · quarter notes (one arpeggio note per beat)
Rule:    clean 3× in a row → Speed Trainer +5 BPM
Ceiling: ~100 BPM, then reset to 60 and switch to eighth notes
Law:     perfect-slow always beats sloppy-fast
```

Speed is the *reward* for accuracy, never the target. If a rep buzzes or fumbles, the tempo was too high — drop 10 BPM without guilt.

---

## Troubleshooting

- **"I can't find it in time."** Good — that's the whole point of the drill; that gap *is* the recall gap. Widen the auto-advance countdown, get 90% clean, then tighten. Don't grind at a countdown that guarantees failure.
- **"I keep mixing up dom7 and maj7."** They differ by *one note* — the 7th. Flip Display to **Degrees** and stare at where the 7 sits vs the ♭7. This confusion is normal and is precisely what Week 2 (guide tones) resolves.
- **"Buzzing / muddy notes."** Tempo's too fast, or you're not letting each arpeggio note ring separately. Slow down; play it *clean and boring* first.
- **"My 5th-string shape feels alien."** Spend an extra minute there. The 5th-string root is the one most players under-drill; it's half of why recall feels slow.

---

## End-of-week checkpoint ✅

You're ready for Week 2 when you can:

- [ ] Name any **m7 / dom7 / maj7** in the 7 keys (C, F, B♭, E♭, A♭, G, D) and land its **root** on the 6th or 5th string in **under ~1 second**.
- [ ] Immediately find its **3rd and 7th** from that root (the guide tones).
- [ ] Play the full arpeggio **ascending *and* descending**, clean, at **~90 BPM**.
- [ ] Hear the difference between the three qualities on the **Chord Quality by Ear** drill more often than not.

Not all four yet? Stay here another few days — Phase 1 is done when the reflex is automatic, not when the calendar says so.
