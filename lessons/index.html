The Recall Gap

Turning theory you already know into recall your hands can reach.

A 12-week plan to build the one missing layer: fast, reflexive recall of chord tones so you can improvise, not just copy. Start with the roadmap for the big picture, then work one weekly lesson at a time.

📋 The full roadmap & method — diagnosis, three pillars, phases, milestones

Phase 1 · Foundations of automaticity

  1. Seventh-Chord Arpeggiosm7 · dom7 · maj7 from the 6th & 5th strings — named → played in under a second.
  2. Guide Tones (3rds & 7ths)Isolate the two notes that define each chord's sound.
  3. Arpeggios Across the NeckConnect positions — any arpeggio, any key, the whole fretboard.
  4. Dorian & Mixolydian Around the ArpeggioThe scale as connective tissue around chord tones.

Phase 2 · Connecting & phrasing

  1. Chord-Tone TargetingLand a chord tone on beat 1 of every chord.
  2. Guide-Tone Lines Through ChangesVoice-lead 3rds & 7ths chord to chord — the smooth-jazz glue.
  3. Chromatic Approach NotesApproach targets from a fret away; enclosures — the bebop flavor.
  4. Motif DevelopmentState a short idea and develop it — melody over scales.

Phase 3 · Freedom & your voice

  1. Vocabulary in All KeysOwn 5 licks in all 12 keys and two positions.
  2. Call & Response / SingingSing it, then find it — close the ear-to-hands gap.
  3. Space, Tone & DynamicsHalf the notes — rests, bends, vibrato, touch.
  4. Solo Over a Real Song, UnscriptedImprovise a full chorus over a tune you know — the proof.
